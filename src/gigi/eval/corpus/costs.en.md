# Cost tracking

Every Monday the finance bot posts the projected monthly spend to `#costs`.
The number comes from the cloud bill exports plus the registry metrics, and is
compared to the budget set per quarter.

Review the invoice list at the end of each month and chase any line item that
is not tagged with a cost centre. Larger than expected charges (more than 10%
over budget for a service) need a short note in the issue tracker before the
review.

The budget spreadsheet lives in Drive; only finance and the platform lead can
edit it.
